Alex
McKelley

Creative Director with 8+ years leading creative vision across games, immersive installations, AI-driven production, and brand campaigns. I own projects from concept to delivery, setting the creative strategy, directing cross-functional teams, and staying hands-on through final execution. My art direction has shaped published game covers for major franchises, editorial print campaigns, and AR/VR experiences featured in major cultural venues and international press. Technically fluent across the full pipeline: real-time environments in Unreal Engine 5 and Unity, custom shaders and game trigger systems in Python, and generative AI pipelines running actively in production. I bring a sharp creative point of view, the range to execute it at every level, and a track record of raising the quality bar on every project I touch.
